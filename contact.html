Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Do you offer home delivery?

Currently, we don't have in-house delivery, but you can order via WhatsApp and we'll help coordinate with local delivery services. Contact us for details!

Can I book the cafe for private events?

Yes! We offer private event bookings for birthdays, bridal showers, corporate meetings, and more. Reach out via the contact form or WhatsApp for availability.

Do you cater for bulk cookie orders?

Absolutely! We take bulk orders for cookie boxes, gifting, and corporate events. Minimum 24 hours notice required. WhatsApp us for pricing.

Is there WiFi available at the cafe?

Yes, we have complimentary high-speed WiFi for all guests. Just ask our staff for the password when you arrive!
