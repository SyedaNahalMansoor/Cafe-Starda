Our Story

Brewed with Heart

From a small dream in Clifton to one of Karachi's most beloved cafes — this is the story of Cafe Starda.

How We Started

From a Passion to a Place

Cafe Starda was born in 2018 from the dream of two coffee-loving friends who believed Karachi deserved a truly world-class café experience — a space that felt like home, smelled like heaven, and tasted even better.

We started with just five tables and a second-hand espresso machine. Today, Cafe Starda is a thriving community hub where students, professionals, families, and coffee lovers of all kinds come together over exceptional brews and freshly baked treats.

Every bean we use is carefully sourced from ethical farms. Every recipe is perfected over dozens of trials. Because we believe you deserve nothing less than the best.
